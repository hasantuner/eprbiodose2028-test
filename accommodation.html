BUBYO Applied Hotel

 

BUBYO Applied Hotel is the official conference hotel for EPRBioDose2028. Located in Ören, Burhaniye, the hotel is affiliated with Balıkesir University and offers accommodation, conference and meeting facilities at the same venue.

 

The hotel has a total of 140 rooms, including standard and suite rooms. Standard rooms are 27 m² and accommodate up to three guests, while 57 m² suite rooms can accommodate up to five guests.

 

For room details, facilities and further information, please visit the official BUBYO Applied Hotel website .

 
 

Accommodation for Registered Participants

Five nights of accommodation, from 10 to 15 September 2028 (check-out on 15 September), are included in the conference registration fee. Therefore, registered participants do not need to make a separate hotel reservation for these nights.

 

Accommodation for Accompanying Persons

Five nights of accommodation, from 10 to 15 September 2028 (check-out on 15 September), are also covered by the accompanying-person registration fee. The applicable shared-room accommodation discount is reflected in the accompanying-person registration fee. No separate hotel reservation is required for these nights.

 

Accompanying persons must indicate the name of the registered participant with whom they intend to share a room in the registration form. This information is essential for room allocation and for calculating the applicable shared-room accommodation rate.

 

Affordable Accommodation at a KYK Student Dormitory

 

An affordable accommodation option is available at a KYK student dormitory, primarily for students.

 

Students and other registered participants who prefer this alternative to the official conference hotel are welcome to contact us for further information and accommodation arrangements.

 
 

Participants who choose to stay at the KYK student dormitory instead of the official conference hotel will be refunded the difference between the accommodation amount included in their registration fee and the actual dormitory accommodation cost during the conference.

 

Arriving Early or Staying Longer?

 

Participants wishing to arrive before 10 September or extend their stay beyond 15 September 2028 are welcome to contact the organizing committee for assistance with additional accommodation arrangements.
