History of EPRBioDose

The History of EPRBioDose

The history of EPRBioDose dates back to 1985, when the first International Symposium on ESR Dating was held in Japan. Initially, the conference series focused on electron spin resonance (ESR), now commonly referred to as electron paramagnetic resonance (EPR), and its applications in dating and dosimetry.

An important development took place in 1998, when the scope of the symposium was broadened to include biological dosimetry through the International Conference on Biological Dosimetry. This brought together two complementary scientific fields under a common conference framework: EPR dosimetry and dating, and biological dosimetry. The combined meetings subsequently developed into the international EPRBioDose conference series.

The scientific scope of EPRBioDose encompasses both fundamental and applied research, including advances in EPR dosimetry, EPR dating, and biological dosimetry. Over the years, the conferences have provided an international forum for presenting new findings, exchanging methodologies, and fostering collaboration among researchers working in these fields.

2010: The Establishment of IABERD

A major milestone in the development of the conference series occurred in 2010, when the EPRBioDose meeting in Mandelieu-La Napoule, France, marked the establishment of the International Association of Biological and EPR Radiation Dosimetry (IABERD).

IABERD was established to provide a common organizational framework for the EPRBioDose conferences and to sustain long-term cooperation among scientists working in EPR dosimetry, EPR dating, and biological dosimetry. Its aims include supporting scientific, technical, and financial cooperation, facilitating the organization of future conferences, and promoting specialized scientific and technical activities.
