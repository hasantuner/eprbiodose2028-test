Abstract Information

Download the abstract template here for instructions on how to format and submit your abstract. Abstract submission deadline is 15 May 2028. The deadline for travel award consideration is 15 April 2028. Abstracts will be approved as they are submitted.

 

Travel Awards

  • Must submit Abstract before 14 April 2028 to be eligible
  • Winners will be announced on 15 May 2028

Student Award

  • All students are eligible to apply, provided they are enrolled at the time of the conference (for example, those graduating before September 2028 are not eligible for the Student Award but may apply for the Early Career Scientist Award).
  • To compete, please submit your abstract with the subject line “EPRBioDose2028 abstract–Student–Surname”, and include a letter of support from the supervisor stating the university affiliation and year of study in order to confirm student status

Early Career Scientist Award

  • Applicants must be IABERD members and must be within 8 years of obtaining their last degree at the time of the conference.
  • To compete, please submit your abstract with the subject line “EPRBioDose2028 abstract–ECS–Surname”
