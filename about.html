EPRBioDose2028

We, the Local Organizing Committee (LOC) of EPRBioDose2028, are pleased to announce that the next EPRBioDose conference will be held in person at Balıkesir University’s BUBYO Applied Hotel in Burhaniye, Balıkesir, Türkiye, from 11 to 15 September 2028. Please save the dates! We look forward to welcoming you to Balıkesir!

 

EPRBioDose is an international conference series organized periodically, every two to three years, by the International Association of Biological and EPR Radiation Dosimetry (IABERD). The conference brings together researchers, professionals, and students working in biological dosimetry, electron paramagnetic resonance (EPR) dosimetry, and EPR dating, providing a forum for scientific exchange and interdisciplinary collaboration.

 

The scientific programme will include invited lectures, oral presentations, and poster sessions covering recent developments in these fields. Abstracts submitted to EPRBioDose2028 will be evaluated by the Scientific Committee through a peer-review process. The proceedings of the conference will also be published in a peer-reviewed international journal. More information will be provided soon.
