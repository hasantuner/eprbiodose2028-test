Welcome to EPRBioDose2028, Balıkesir

The EPRBioDose2028 Balıkesir is in-person conference organized by the International Association of Biological and EPR Radiation Dosimetry (IABERD), and hosted by Balıkesir University. The aim of IABERD is to stimulate and coordinate biological and EPR radiation dosimetry activities around the world. The meeting will incorporate posters and oral presentations from the biological and EPR radiation dosimetry, and EPR dating community. Our conference encompasses a wide range of topics, bridging the realms of EPR dosimetry, EPR dating, and biological dosimetry applied to ionizing radiation. The meeting will provide a balance between fundamental research and practical applications, showcasing new data and diagnostic strategies in the frame of the management of overexposures to ionizing radiation for a full spectrum of events.



On behalf of the Local Organizing Committee, we cordially invite you to join us for EPRBioDose2028 in Balıkesir, Türkiye, from 11 to 15 September 2028.
