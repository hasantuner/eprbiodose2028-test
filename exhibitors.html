Become an Exhibitor at EPRBioDose2026

IABERD invites your organization to present its products, services, and cutting-edge innovations to an international audience of leaders and decision-makers in radiation biology, biodosimetry, and radiation protection. The EPRBioDose2026 conference aims to attract a diverse audience, including students, professionals, and experts engaged in both the fundamentals and applications of biological dosimetry, EPR dosimetry and dating.

 

Please reach out to us at EPRBioDose2026+exhibitor@gmail.com for all exhibitor inquiries.

Exhibit Space

Exhibit space can be found in Les Saisons on the 3rd floor of the Westin Hotel. This room is shared with the poster presentations and is on the same floor and adjacent to the main conference hall.

Booths

The exhibit space can accommodate 10 8’by 8’ booths. Each booth includes a 6’ table and 2 chairs, electrical service and Wi-Fi. Please contact us for additional equipment requests.
