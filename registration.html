Important note

  • By attending this event, you agree that you may appear in photographs and/or videos that may be used for the public relations and promotional activities of IABERD and Balıkesir University.
 

Refund Policy

  • 100% refund until August 16, 2028.
  • 50% refund between August 16 and September 10, 2028.
  • No refund after September 10, 2028.
  • Please note that any refund will be subject to an applicable service charge.
 

Travel Awards

  • Must submit Abstract before April 14th, 2028 to be eligible
  • Winners will be announced on May 15th, 2028

Student Award

  • Students are eligible only if they remain enrolled at the time of the conference. Those who graduate before September 2028 are not eligible for the Student Award but may apply for the Early Career Scientist Award.
  • A supervisor's support letter is required, confirming university affiliation and year of study.
  • An abstract submission is required. To compete, please submit your abstract with the subject line “EPRBioDose2028 abstract–Student–surname”,

Early Career Scientist Award

  • Applicants must be IABERD members and must be within 8 years of obtaining their last degree at the time of the conference.
  • An abstract submission is required. To compete, please submit your abstract with the subject line “EPRBioDose2028 abstract–ECS–surname”
