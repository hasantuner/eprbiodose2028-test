Conference Venue

The EPR BioDose 2028 Conference will take place at: The BUBYO Applied Hotel in Burhaniye, Balıkesir, Türkiye

Address: Ören, Enver Güreli Cd. No:82/1, 10700 Burhaniye/Balıkesir, Türkiye

From Istanbul Airport (IST), there are two main travel options to reach BUBYO Uygulama Oteli in Burhaniye:

Air Travel

  • Istanbul Airport (IST) → Balıkesir Koca Seyit Airport (EDO)
    • Turkish Airlines operates direct flights from Istanbul Airport (IST) to Balıkesir Koca Seyit Airport (EDO).
    • The flight takes approximately 1 hour.
    • Estimated airfare: approximately €45–50 one way, before applying any applicable Turkish Airlines event discount.
    • The transfer from EDO to BUBYO is not included in this estimate.


  • EDO → BUBYO
    • BUBYO can be reached from the airport by taxi, private transfer or rental car.

Google Maps – EDO to BUBYO

Land Travel

  • Istanbul Airport (IST) → Balıkesir Bus Terminal → Burhaniye Bus Terminal
    • İstanbul Seyahat operates direct bus services from Istanbul Airport (IST) to Balıkesir Bus Terminal.
      (At Istanbul Airport, follow the signs for the Intercity Bus / Coach Transportation area and look for the İstanbul Seyahat Havalimanı counter/boarding point. The exact departure platform may change; please check the airport information screens on the day of travel.)
    • The journey from Istanbul Airport to Balıkesir Bus Terminal takes approximately 4.5 hours.
    • From Balıkesir Bus Terminal, regular intercity buses are available to Burhaniye Bus Terminal.
    • The total estimated cost is approximately €30–35, excluding the final transfer from Burhaniye Bus Terminal to BUBYO.


  • Istanbul Airport (IST) → Esenler Bus Terminal → Burhaniye Bus Terminal
    • From Istanbul Airport, take the HAVAİST shuttle to Esenler Bus Terminal.
      (At Istanbul Airport, take the HAVAİST shuttle to Esenler Bus Terminal. Please check the HAVAİST information screens or the HAVAİST website for the current departure platform and schedule.)
    • Several intercity bus companies operate direct services from Esenler Bus Terminal to Burhaniye.
    • The bus journey from Esenler to Burhaniye takes approximately 7–8 hours, depending on the service and traffic conditions.
    • The total estimated cost is approximately €30–35, excluding the final transfer from Burhaniye Bus Terminal to BUBYO.


  • Burhaniye Bus Terminal → BUBYO
    • BUBYO is only a few kilometres from Burhaniye Bus Terminal.
    • Taxis are available at the bus terminal.
