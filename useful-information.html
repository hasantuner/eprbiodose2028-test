Visa information

Türkiye offers visa exemption for citizens of many countries, often for stays of up to 90 days. However, visa requirements and permitted length of stay vary depending on nationality and passport type.

To check the visa requirements applicable to your nationality, please visit the official Visa Information for Foreigners page of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs of the Republic of Türkiye.

Participants who are required to obtain a visa should follow the application procedure specified for their nationality. For conference attendance, please check the applicable visa category and application requirements before making your travel arrangements.

Eligible travelers may apply for an electronic visa (e-Visa) through the official Turkish e-Visa portal. Please note that the e-Visa is intended for tourism or commercial purposes; participants traveling to Türkiye for other purposes should follow the visa procedure indicated by the Turkish authorities.

Participants are advised to check the visa requirements well in advance of their planned travel. Passport validity and other entry requirements may also vary according to nationality.

 

Currency

The official currency of Türkiye is the Turkish lira (₺, TRY). Major credit and debit cards are widely accepted at hotels, restaurants, shops and other establishments.

 

Telephone and Internet

Türkiye’s international country code is +90. SIM cards and eSIM services are available from major telecommunications providers and can be purchased at airports, authorized stores and online. Free Wi-Fi is commonly available at hotels, cafés and some public places.

 

Electricity

The electrical power supply in Türkiye is 220 V, 50 Hz, AC. The standard power plugs are Type C and Type F.
